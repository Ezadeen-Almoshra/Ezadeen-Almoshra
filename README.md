## About

I am Ezadeen Mansour Almoshra, a full stack software engineer based in Taiz, Yemen. I studied information technology at Taizz University from 2019 to 2023 and graduated with a very good grade.

I build SaaS, e-commerce, e-invoicing, and fleet platforms. Most of my work is in React, Next.js, NestJS, and Node.js, with PostgreSQL and SQL Server, then I put it on Ubuntu with Nginx, PM2, Docker, and CI/CD.

From September 2024 to September 2026 I worked full-time at SEQA Group on the group's internal platforms. Before that, from January 2022 to 2024, I delivered web, mobile, and commerce products on a contract basis, including Todawini, EWA Insurance, and a real-time chat app.

Arabic is my first language, and I also work in English.

## Experience

**Full Stack Software Engineer · SEQA Group**  
September 2024 – September 2026 · Full-time

Full-stack work on the group's internal platforms, with Next.js, NestJS, and Express, PostgreSQL and SQL Server, and deployment on the server.

- Electronic invoicing that signs invoices and submits them to the Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority (ZATCA).
- A multi-brand store for bottled-water delivery, with orders, subscriptions, live tracking, and tax invoicing.
- Treasury handling for bank facilities, loans, installment schedules, and charges, with approvals and posting to the ERP.
- Journal entries between companies in the group, with currency conversion and posting to SAP Business One.
- Shared master records and financial tickets, with configurable forms and multi-step approvals.
- In-store retail sales, with catalog, shifts, invoicing, and a link to SAP and e-invoicing.
- Production hosting with Nginx, PM2, SSL, and UFW.

Integrations: PayTabs, Paymob, ZATCA, SAP Business One, Qoyod, Google Maps, Firebase, SMS OTP, AWS S3, Cloudflare R2, Office 365, Google reCAPTCHA.
